Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This policy applies to the personal Discord bots operated by Phillip Jubb (operating as philjubb). These bots are personal, homelab-hosted automation tools run for the operator's own use and that of selected users.

Data we collect

These bots process only the messages, commands, and attachments that users voluntarily send them in order to respond. No data is collected passively, and no analytics or tracking is performed.

How data is used

  • Messages are processed locally within the operator's own infrastructure to carry out the requested task (e.g. retrieving data, running a command).
  • Conversation history is stored locally to maintain context between messages. It is not transmitted to any third party.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not bulk-message or spam any user.

Retention

Conversation logs are retained only as long as needed for the bot to function and are deleted with the local state of the bot. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Contact

To ask questions or request deletion, contact the operator via the Discord server where the bot is used, or by email to the operator's personal address.
